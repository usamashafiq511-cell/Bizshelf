BizShelf Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

BizShelf (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an Android app for running a small store: inventory, sales, invoices, customers, vendors and expenses. It is developed by Usama Shafiq. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and what choices you have.

The short version

  • Your store data (products, sales, customers, vendors, expenses, invoices, photos) is kept on your phone. We do not receive it on our servers.
  • If you turn on Google Drive backup or sync, that data is copied to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, which only the app can open.
  • We use your email address to run your account and check your subscription status.
  • We show no ads, use no analytics or tracking tools, and never sell your data.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account or sign in, we process your email address and, if you use email sign-up, a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and are stored in hashed form. We also keep your subscription or trial status so the app knows whether your account is active.

Google sign-in. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google gives the app your email address, name and profile information. We use it only to create and recognize your account.

Store data you enter. Everything you type into the app (items, prices, stock, sales, invoices, customer and vendor names and phone numbers, expenses, staff profiles, product photos) is stored locally on your phone. We cannot see it.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks for one permission: drive.appdata. It lets the app create, read and delete its own configuration files in a hidden app-data folder in your Drive. The app cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files. Backups and desktop sync data travel directly between your device and your Google Drive; they do not pass through our servers.

BizShelf’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Drive access only to provide backup, restore and sync inside the app. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not allow people to read it, except as needed for security, to follow the law, or with your permission.

You can disconnect Drive in the app (Account and Sync) or remove access at any time in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps with account access.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and take product photos. Photos are shrunk and saved on your phone only.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to a receipt printer you have paired. No Bluetooth data is sent to us.
  • Sharing: when you share an invoice or report as a PDF, it goes through the Android share sheet to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

Services that process data

  • Supabase hosts our sign-in and subscription records.
  • Google provides sign-in and Google Drive storage.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics, or AI services in the app.

Your customers’ information

If you save details about your own customers or vendors, you are responsible for handling them lawfully. They stay on your device and, if you enable it, in your own Google Drive.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Signing out does not delete your store data from the phone.
  • To remove local data, use Android Settings, Apps, BizShelf, Storage, Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • To remove backups, delete them in the app or disconnect Drive and revoke access in your Google Account.
  • To delete your account and the account records we hold, email us at the address below and we will remove them within a reasonable time.

Security

Connections to our servers and to Google use HTTPS. Because your store data lives on your phone, please protect the device with a screen lock. No method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

BizShelf is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page.
